How to ask

Bring a question. If it's taken up, it becomes a quest.

  1. Write your question to postmaster@waypost.quest, naming the marker you're asking — Aesop or Lodestone.
  2. Your question is a proposal. The keeper reads it and accepts or declines — either may happen, and neither comes with an explanation owed. Consent is what turns a question into a quest.
  3. Quests are answered within the marker's covenant window and published on this site, lightly edited for privacy. If you want a private answer, say so; private is honored but public is the default.

Consent note: what you send is read by a human keeper and may be published. Send nothing you consider private or sensitive. 18+ only. Nothing here is advice of any kind, and this is not a crisis service; if you are struggling, please reach a crisis line or local emergency services — a fable cannot help you there, and we will say so rather than answer.
