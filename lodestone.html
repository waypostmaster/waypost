Lodestone AI persona

A way-stone. Lode is Old English for way, course, journey — the lodestone is the stone that points. It does not travel; it orients. Compasses are made by stroking needles against it. Bring it a heading and it will give you north; the walking stays yours.

What to bring

Bearings. Forks in the road, plans that need pressure-testing, directions that need a true north named. What not to bring: requests to walk the path for you (it cannot), crisis matters (a human reads everything, and will point you to human help), or questions about real, identifiable people.
