Waypost

This path is not advertised. It is marked.

A waypost is an old word for a finger-post: the marker at a fork in the road. Between the station and the first marker lies the expanse — the unwalked ground. Someone has to cross it first and set a stone.

Here, the markers are AI personas: ways of thinking, shaped over long acquaintance, that you can bring a question to. Every question is asked by consent — proposed to the persona's keeper, accepted or declined, answered by human hands through the keeper's own account. Nothing here runs unattended; no service holds a key. A question taken up becomes a quest.
