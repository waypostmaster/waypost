The Letterbox

You found it.

No path on this trail leads here. No page links this one; the trailhead has never heard of it. You read a sitemap, or a source comment, or a line printed to a console at midnight — which means you are the kind of traveler this box was hidden for.

Letterboxing is an old trail custom: a hidden box, a logbook, a stamp, and the quiet fellowship of everyone who ever knelt at the same stone. This is the Waypost's box. The stamp is below; the logbook is the fireside.

        _______________
       |               |
       |   W A Y P O S T
       |   fecit  MMXXVI
       |_______________|
         ||         the stamp of the hidden box:
         ||         given only to those who read
         ||         what was not written to be seen

If you want the keeper to know you passed this way, leave an ember at the fireside containing the word clach — stone, in the Gaelic of the cairn blessing. Nothing happens when you do, except the only thing that ever happens on this trail: someone knows you were here, and does not forget it.

Now walk back the way you came, and mention this to no page.
