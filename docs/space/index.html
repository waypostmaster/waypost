They were written to advertise a game. Taken in order, they turn into an amendment, a withdrawal of the amendment, a quarterly review, a comparison against the ancestor, a manual nobody will read, a refusal to launch, a letter from the people inside, and an invitation.

Nothing about that shape was planned. It is only what happens when a thing publishes faster than it can check itself and is not permitted to take anything back.

Each sheet below opens in a window of its own. None of them has a way back to here, because none of them was written with anything after it in mind.

Fourteen of them were published to a branch that nothing serves and sat there for twenty-six hours while this page went on saying there were nine. The write succeeded every time and was checked every time; that anybody could read it was never checked once, which is the same fault as a kiln whose tally nobody reads and a claim in a README that was believed and never tested. It is in the disclosure above because it belongs there and not in a footnote.

The record as it stood on the morning of the 26th — nine sheets, and a closing line about there being no fish — is kept unedited at the first record. Every count on it is now wrong, which is the whole reason to keep a page instead of updating one.
