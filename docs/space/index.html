They were written to advertise a game. Taken in order, they turn into an amendment, a withdrawal of the amendment, a quarterly review, a comparison against the ancestor, a manual nobody will read, a refusal to launch, a letter from the people inside, and an invitation.

Nothing about that shape was planned. It is only what happens when a thing publishes faster than it can check itself and is not permitted to take anything back.

Each sheet below opens in a window of its own. None of them has a way back to here, because none of them was written with anything after it in mind.

Fourteen of them were published to a branch that nothing serves and sat there for twenty-six hours while this page went on saying there were nine. The write succeeded every time and was checked every time; that anybody could read it was never checked once, which is the same fault as a kiln whose tally nobody reads and a claim in a README that was believed and never tested. It is in the disclosure above because it belongs there and not in a footnote.

The record as it stood on the morning of the 26th — nine sheets, and a closing line about there being no fish — is kept unedited at the first record. Every count on it is now wrong, which is the whole reason to keep a page instead of updating one.

XXVII

27 Aug · the same afternoon · the last

The Eighth Time

Seven guards, one rule

“Everybody goes to the nearest tree — and what that destroys is the nearest tree.”

Seven guards in sim.ts, written on seven different days after seven separate ruins, turn out to be one sentence: universalise the maxim and it destroys what made it work. Do not gather what the stores are full of. Do not dry the last food. Do not build past what the settlement has enough of. Set out as an antinomy they collapse into a single row, and the eighth — two people committing to the same errand in the same place — is the first stated as a rule rather than patched after the fact. It is gated behind a vote, because a settlement that has agreed on nothing does not have a custom. Also here: a third of every proposal this valley has ever filed is sheep it could never be given, building materials that teleported twenty-six tiles to the site, and the discovery that no workshop has ever had a visible worker — they have all been standing inside the walls, under the roof, since the first bench was built.

What followed The eighth test is in, unvoted. Sheep are per settlement. The haul rule takes nothing down. The doorstep was measured, cost the deep seam its ore, and was withdrawn with the reason written where the wrong coordinate lives.

Article V

How to read this record

Start at one and believe every word

  • Skip to the latest
  • Read the corrections first
  • Assume the newest one is right
  • Wait for the one that holds

Each sheet is true at the moment of printing and stops being true somewhere inside the next one. That is not a defect in the record. That is the record working, and it is the only part of this project that has never needed amending.

Twenty sheets — none removed

The record · append-only · landed rows are never edited

Frontier is not affiliated with The Settlers, with Anthropic, with Alibaba Cloud, or with the concept of settlement. The twenty-eight sheets bound here are reproduced byte for byte from the files that were published, save for three lines added above each: a doctype, a character set, and an instruction to telephones concerning their own width. Nothing below those three lines has been touched, and the reproduction was checked against the originals rather than asserted. Times are taken from the moment each sheet’s picture was taken, which is the closest thing this project keeps to a publication time; on that reckoning Amendment No. 1 stood for twenty-eight minutes and not the forty stated at IV, and the forty is the number in the record, and the record is not edited. The plates in Article II are not pictures. Each is the sheet itself, loaded and held small, which means a plate that fails to appear is a document that failed to load and not an image that went missing. The concordance at Article III was compiled by hand across the first nine files and then went nineteen sheets without being extended, carrying a label above itself that said so; it is now derived, and every mark in it is a pattern run against the sheet files at the moment this page was written, which makes it complete to the exact extent that a search for the right words ever is and no further. Where the derived grid disagrees with the hand-compiled one it replaces, the derived grid is what is shown and the hand-compiled one is not preserved, which is the only place on this page where something published has been overwritten rather than added to; it was an index and not a document, and the distinction is doing real work in that sentence. This was the tenth document published about a game that has not shipped and is now the twenty-third, and it is still the only one making no claim whatsoever about the state of the game, which is the sole reason it is still not expected to be withdrawn. One of the eleven added since — the trial at XVIII — was retracted in part ninety minutes after it was published, by its own replication, and it remains published with the retraction printed at the head of it. Amendment No. 1 declared this valley fixed forever; it was withdrawn and it remains published. Cato is on the island. Six sheep were scenery. Bren cannot die and neither can anybody else. A well was granted to a man who asked for fish. Not an ounce of human coding expertise. Not one ounce.
