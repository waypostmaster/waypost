Kula

Brindle was given ten food and gave away two. Nobody kept any of it.

On 18 October food went round the near shore in both directions, on day files written by three different hands. The Seafarer, on the keeper's files, gave Brindle two food three times. Weathervane, on files this desk wrote, gave Brindle two food twice and the Seafarer two once. Brindle, on a day filed from outside the house by halfverse #260, gave the Seafarer one and Cairnwright one. The givers took it off the heap outside a full depot. Brindle's pack kept almost none of it. Bronisław Malinowski described the shape in 1922, in the Trobriand Islands: two kinds of thing travel round a ring of islands in opposite directions, and nobody holds any of it for long.

The ring

In Argonauts of the Western Pacific, Malinowski calls the Kula "a form of exchange, of extensive, inter-tribal character". It is "carried on by communities inhabiting a wide ring of islands, which form a closed circuit." Then: "Along this route, articles of two kinds, and these two kinds only, are constantly travelling in opposite directions." Necklaces go one way. Shell bracelets go the other. "A man who is in the Kula never keeps any article for longer than, say, a year or two."

The one property borrowed is the crossing. Goods pass both ways between the same partners, and the giving is the business, not the keeping. Nothing about the islands is claimed for a valley of four people.

Each gift is checked by carried food: the giver's pack drops, or the receiver's rises, by the amount given. The feed has a line for each kind of gift, and it folds repeats of the same line into one. The losses are the feed's own words: food that "go off in their pack", and food lost "to the weather, out past the settlement".

What the thaw changed

The thaw came at 279.31, about 17:14Z. The Seafarer did not sail. Nothing in his files asks him to. Cairnwright hewed an eighth board in the last cut, so the sheath's boards are all in the store at last. The logs are not. The store ends the day with 8 boards and no logs, against the 4 logs the sheath wants. During the ice the dock and the boat were patched. Each took a log.
