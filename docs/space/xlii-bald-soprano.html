The Bald Soprano

On fourteen settlers who asked for a mechanism and were handed a shed, a ledger, a man up a tower and a furnace — read through Ionesco, whose clock strikes seventeen and whose title names somebody who is not in the play.

Paris, 1950 · run mtdotjm9-e4 14 proposals · 9 kinds · 0 of them right
The finding Every trade on this coast either pulls something out of the ground or raises a structure. Nobody makes a thing. So when a settler asks for one, the catalogue puts it in whichever bin is nearest by vocabulary — and the vocabulary is not about mechanisms, so the answers come out as comedy. Fourteen proposals, nine kinds, and not one of them landed on what was asked for. Two settlers asked to keep time and were handed the debt book and the forge, and the two asks did not even land in the same bin as each other.

II The clock strikes seventeen

MRS SMITH: There, it is nine o’clock. (The clock strikes seven times. Silence. The clock strikes three times. Silence. The clock does not strike.) Ionesco, La Cantatrice chauve, 1950

Ionesco wrote it after trying to learn English from a phrasebook and finding that the sentences, all perfectly correct, added up to nothing anybody could mean. The clock strikes whatever it strikes. The title names a bald soprano who never appears and is mentioned once, by mistake, by a fireman.

Which is the shape of this exactly. Nothing in the catalogue is broken. Every word in it is a real word with a real grant behind it, every classification is consistent, and check-lexicon and check-record both pass the whole set: 0 open misfiles, 0 unheard, 2,252 negation pairs correct. The sentences are all correct. It is the meaning that has nowhere to go.

This is the class Sheet XLI could only name with one specimen — heard confidently as the thing the settler said in order to rule it out. Fourteen specimens now, and it is not an edge case. It is what a catalogue does when it has no bin for a whole kind of want.

III What was missing was a trade

Fourteen mechanisms is not fourteen features anybody wanted. It is one wish asked fourteen times, and the wish is for a person: somebody who makes things that are not buildings. There has never been such a trade here, so there was never a word, so there was never a bin.

So there is a tinker now, and the vote grants a bench and nothing else. Every mechanism after it costs boards, iron and a shift, made by somebody standing at it — which is the rule the roads boon was breaking one sheet ago and the reason this grant is not another invisible percentage.

  1. A pulley and sheer-legs 3 plank · 2 iron · one shift — for wish-32 A load that used to want two trips wants one. read in capacityOf() — +3, and it stacks with a cart, because a cart and a block are different things
  2. Drying racks 4 plank · one shift — for wish-358, 473, 493 What is stacked outside no longer goes off before anybody can carry it in. read in outsideStep() — the heap spoils at 0.4×
  3. A bell on a rope 2 plank · 2 iron · one shift — for wish-152, 155, 321 A call for help is heard from the far side of the workings. read in the hearing radius — +90, whether or not anybody is looking your way
  4. A sundial 4 stone · 2 plank · one shift — for wish-384 and wish-623 People stop because it is evening, rather than because they fell over. read where a settler decides to rest — 0.88 becomes 0.74

The sundial is the one worth stating plainly. hourLabel has existed in this game for its whole life, is drawn on the HUD and handed to every model in the state file, and nothing in the simulation has ever read it. Perrin asked for a clock so we don’t have to wait for the sun to tell us. What a settlement gets is not the hour. It is the thing knowing the hour is actually for: stopping at one.

And then the thing worth the whole sheet. The moment the word existed, check-lexicon re-read the back catalogue and found wish-384 and wish-623 now reading as tinker — it caught its own two worst misfilings retroactively, and refused to pass until the open one was corrected. The sundial had already carried under ledger, so that vote stands, as every vote here does. The clock is refiled.
