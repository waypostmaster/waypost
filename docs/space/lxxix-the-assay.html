The Assay

Before anyone sinks a shaft, the assay office runs a spoonful of the ore through a crucible small enough to watch. The little furnace is not the mine and never pretends to be — its product is a number and a residue: how much the rock holds, and exactly which flux the real smelter is going to run short of. The ninth valley was the record’s assay — four composed hands, every mechanism legible at that scale — and it is read here for closing with both results in hand: the founding method runs true, and the flux that is missing has a name.

The finding The articles assay true, and the missing flux is iron. Every founder executed their signed character to the letter — the secession, the repayment, the industry, all of it traceable to sentences committed before the world existed. The bed question, which founded two towns in the eighth trial, closed here for good: nobody slept on the ground after tick 885. And then the whole valley, both jurisdictions, walked into the same wall and said so in one sentence, eight times: “a saw is a blade, and nobody here has iron to make one from.” The sawmill’s pegs came up seven times across two towns; every statute either town carried — granary, store, shelter, carts, dry food — waits on boards; boards wait on the mill; the mill waits on a blade nobody can draw. The eighth trial reached iron with thirty-five settlers. The ninth measured the other end: four hands can reach the kiln and cannot cross the saw. That number is the assay’s product, and the tenth valley sails with five because of it.

I Why a spoonful

The pilot plant — Sheet LXXV — ran thirty-five settlers for seventy-two valley days and produced findings only scale can produce: the weather as the biggest employer nobody hired, secession’s single cause, ninety-candidate slates working and unreadable. What it could not produce is legibility. At thirty-five, a vote is a count; at four, a vote is four people you know by name, and you know why each of them raised a hand. Every debt in the ninth valley is attributable, every collapse has a spoken sentence next to it, and every statute’s failure names the exact rung it starved on. That is what an assay buys: not truth at scale but truth per gram. Twenty-seven ballots were counted and all of them can be read as biography — nine carried in a jurisdiction whose whole electorate is one woman, six at a landing where every majority is two of three faces at a fireside.

The record can now say what Sheet LXXVII could not see yet: the articles came true mechanically, clause by clause. Her file said she had built three houses for other people and wanted, once, to build one that was hers — and the run had her build the landing’s three, then refused her the fourth, and the one house she was denied was precisely the one the file said she boarded for. So she built it in her own jurisdiction, then built a kiln beside it — a one-woman town with heavy industry, nine statutes, and a charcoal economy waiting on its first burn. And the debt arc, which LXXVII left open at its worst — done in, eating on credit from the town she walked out of — closed in the only direction that matters: at tick 2,428 the ledger reads Edda gave 1 food to Wren, against what they owed. The woman the valley owed everything, who then owed it dinner, paid it back. The jurisdiction of one assays as: can house itself, can industrialise, can legislate, cannot farm, and settles its accounts.

III The iron wall

From tick 2,268 to the reading, the log is a fugue on one subject. Edda stakes the sawmill; the pegs come up; she states the reason in the game’s own arithmetic — a mill wants a saw, a saw is a blade, a blade is iron, and the valley’s whole iron economy is six ore sitting at the landing and four at the claim with no smelter, no charcoal burnt, and no hands to spare for the hill. Tam staked a sawmill at the landing; its pegs came up too. Sorrel’s came up third. Three separate people reached independently for the same rung and none of them could touch it, because the rung is not a decision, it is a head count: somebody must dig, somebody must burn, somebody must draw, and somebody must still feed all three — and there are four people in the world. The eighth trial proved iron reachable at thirty-five; the ninth proves it out of reach at four with the food margin this coast allows. The minimum crew for the metal age lies somewhere in between, and the record now brackets it from both sides — which is what assays are for.

IV The cost ledger, and the boat

The boat has gone to pieces at last. Whatever this place becomes, it is home now. the feed, tick 2,390 — the ninth valley’s own closing sentence, filed before anyone asked for one

The assay’s heat shows in the crucible walls. In the last five hundred ticks of the reading, all three landing hands dropped where they stood — Tam at 2,311, Wren at 2,371, Sorrel at 2,407, each days since they last slept — and Tam was hurt when a frame shifted while he was under it. Four hands are enough to live and not enough to rest: every errand is somebody’s whole trade, the food stores closed at two, and the only farmer spent her margins walking stone to a town of one. Nobody died, nobody starved, nobody slept rough — and nobody, for the last thousand ticks, was ever more than one bad day from all three. Then at 2,390 the boat — whose company was named and closed on the water, the first physics the founding ever set — went to pieces on the shingle, and the feed wrote the valley’s epitaph in advance and called it home.

V The residue, and the tenth

Closing means the run stops and the record does not: 27 ballots, 71 wish-lines, two kilns, four houses, one claim, purse nought in both towns — coin carried by vote in a jurisdiction of one and never minted, the second trial running to end with the epoch legislated and unreached. Thin, and said so: the reading and the closing are hours apart, so every number is a dated floor; four hands are one experiment and five will be another, not a law; and the assay’s central claim — that the wall was hands and not luck — is exactly the kind of assertion this record has retired six of tonight, so it is stated as a bracket, not a constant. The tenth valley’s articles are signed: five hands, five trades — and among them, this time, people whose files remember bread as well as beds, because the ninth’s sharpest lesson was that a manifesto executes its omissions with the same fidelity as its clauses. Their boat takes its vote on the water like the last one; where it lands is theirs, and the sea’s, to say.

Hedge on the crucible: fire assay is a real trade with real standards, and its numbers came with error bars this borrowed shape drops; the narrow part kept is the purpose — a small, watched, expendable run whose product is knowledge about what the full-scale attempt will run short of, and the discipline of writing the shortfall down before sinking the shaft.

Written 30 August, read for closing at tick 2,479 with the valley still running and the log past it; the closing itself waits on the keeper’s hand at the window, so this sheet is written in the record’s oldest tense — prepared-for, rather than done.
