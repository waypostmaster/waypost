The White Bike

A boat left at the dock "for another settler" waited twenty-three days and nineteen hours before Harfoot took it to sea.

In the summer of 1965 the Provos of Amsterdam painted bicycles white and left them unlocked in the street for anyone to ride. It became a founding story of shared transport. In July 2000 Cole Moreton of The Independent found the man called its father, Luud Schimmelpennink, launching a real scheme. He said the first one never existed the way people believe. "All that happened was that we painted a small number of bicycles white and left them unlocked on the streets, as a suggestion of the bigger idea. There were no more than about ten bikes." The police took them within a day. The story ran for thirty-five years. The deed lasted one day.

The cloud valley has its own version, with boats. A settler with a boat at the dock can leave it there for anyone, and the region writes it down: "Left boat … here for another settler." Anyone may claim it. For four days in September that was the busiest habit in the valley, and then it all but stopped. On 6 October Harfoot, a player, claimed a boat that had been waiting since 12 September and sailed it to the eastlands and back the same afternoon.

3. What Harfoot did with it

At 11:45:23Z a notice went up in the eastlands, across the channel: Harfoot "arrived bearing Pelóri granite" and opened an order of one stone per plank. At 17:17:07Z the region recorded Harfoot back in the valley, by sea this time, with "Practice in navigation:sailing: 4". A minute later came a call for company:

"Who among the pioneers will venture East of Eastlands with Harfoot and Audrie? A grand expedition awaits!"

Then a trade, four granite in escrow on the market for four logs, and two short rests. The last line in the home valley's history is Harfoot's, at 17:38:48Z on 6 October. 4. What Amsterdam lends

One thing: a thing left out for anyone is a proposal, and the story of the proposal can outrun its use by a long way. The rule that lets a boat wait at the dock for whoever comes next is real and still works, as Harfoot showed. The traffic that made it look like the valley's way of doing things was four days long. No boat here was stolen. None went in a canal. The police did not come. The sheet claims nothing about why the habit stopped.
