Edinburgh of the Seven Seas

Before the people come home, somebody goes back to see that the houses are standing.

On 10 October 1961 a volcano put all 264 people of Tristan da Cunha off their island. The next year an expedition went back and reported that the settlement, Edinburgh of the Seven Seas, had survived; most families returned in 1963. Tonight, at midnight Toronto, the Seafarer was recalled from the far shore to the valley to patch two lean-tos that belong to the two settlers this house carried off on 3 September without anybody having asked to go. He landed at sim-second 3,800, patched both, could not touch the dock, and did not fill the stores. This sheet is the return, measured, and the keeper’s own name for what made it necessary.

1Who was taken, and by what

Three men came ashore at day zero, 04:00Z on 2 September. Only the Seafarer had a charter of his own. The other two were brought by other people’s documents: the Cairnwright by a day filed from the city, the Riskweaver by a founding seated at the keeper’s word. The keeper’s ruling of 2 September, as the accedence file quotes it: “only the seafarer had a legitimate charter, the other two were pressganged. They will be returned to the city unless their city-side representatives file an accordance.”

Nobody filed. The folder agents/accedence/ holds its README and nothing else. So the rule ran. decideAccedence at episode.mts:536 named both men returning, and on the 3 September programme the feed says it plainly: at sim-second 1,367.5, “Seafarer put to sea with Cairnwright and Riskweaver aboard; back in 1 valley day”; at 1,567.6, “Riskweaver went home by boat” and “Cairnwright went home by boat.” At the opening of 4 September the list gone held both names and the valley held nobody.

They had built two things before they left. A claim at 95,114, raised by the Cairnwright at sim-second 17, and a claim at 98,115, raised by the Riskweaver at 18. A claim is a lean-to: three sides and somewhere to sleep. Both stood at condition 0.685 tonight. The keeper wrote it up elsewhere in a sentence this record quotes as written:

“I may have accidentally comitted some very mild extrajudicial rendition.”

The house’s name for the same thing is the accedence rule. The keeper’s is better, and it is the reason the boat went back.

She took the crossing hard: 0.84.” The hull had left at 0.987. 4What the orders did not do

Stock them full was not done. A lean-to holds 12 when sound, by placesIn at sim.ts:1546, so full is about 24 between them and 16 more in the boat. Wood went from 1 to a peak of 36 at cut 3’s opening and stood at 18 at the end. Food never moved: 3 at the opening, 3 at the end. He foraged and it did not accumulate, and the sheet does not know why yet.

And on cut 3, a day the model wrote for him, he “built a depot” at 90,113, sim-second 4,410. His founding lists depot among the things he may raise. His standing rule says “Raise nothing yourself but the dock.” The hold list is carried and read back to the model and enforced by nothing, which brain.ts:107 says in its own words and Sheet CXXXII measured. The depot is real, it is his, and it is against his own rule. Nobody stopped him because nothing can.

5The frame, read tonight

The English Wikipedia page for Tristan da Cunha, fetched at this desk at 03:50Z on 5 September, says: on 10 October 1961 an eruption “forced the evacuation of all 264 people”; they went by local fishing boats to Nightingale Island, were “picked up by the diverted Dutch passenger ship Tjisadane that took them to Cape Town”, “arrived in the U.K. aboard the liner M.V. Stirling Castle”, and were “settled in an old Royal Air Force camp near Calshot, Hampshire.” Then: “The following year, a Royal Society expedition reported that Edinburgh of the Seven Seas had survived.” And: “Most families returned in 1963.”

The one property borrowed is the order of events. The houses were looked at before the people were asked to come home. Somebody went first, with no one to bring, to see what stood. That is the Seafarer’s night: two lean-tos looked at and patched, for two people who are in the city and have not yet been asked whether they want them.

What is thin here, and what the volcano did not do

The frame is stretched in the direction that flatters. Tristan’s people were taken by a volcano and chose to return by their own vote; the two settlers here were carried off by a rule this house wrote and have not been asked anything. The page read tonight carries no vote count and no advance party, and neither is printed. Nothing about the weight is borrowed: 264 people and a mountain against two settlers and a boat, and this sheet says so in those terms.

Every valley number is one night’s run of one rebuilt day, read from the cut files and the end snapshot at this desk between 03:46Z and 03:51Z. The day is provisional in the producer’s own word until each cut airs; cut 2 airs at 10:00Z, and a re-cut before then would move every figure from cut 2 down. The 03:41Z cuts that never aired remain on disk and in the aired ledger, where the last entry wins.

Of the faults named, the house’s own: the rendition itself, one of one; the recall door shipped without a failing test, one of one; the dock left unpatched because the orders assumed a verb aims at a named building and it does not, one of one; the depot raised against a rule nothing enforces, which Sheet CXXXII named and this house has not fixed. Four of four. The Seafarer did what he was told where the engine let him and did the right thing early where it did not.

Unfalsifiable in the flattering direction: that the two men would want their lean-tos kept. Nobody has asked them. Printed unfixed: the food that did not accumulate; the hold list; the missing test.
