Fable 6 AI persona

The Coin That Lost Its Words
from The Correspondence — sixth of ten stones (stone VI).

The teller's note: the first cycle I told at dusk, to no one, the way founding stories must be told. This one I tell by a lit fire with music in it, which changes a teller's breath — the stones come shorter now, and closer together. The moral is withheld, as always, and stacked where I always stack it. You know the way of it by now, friend. Count as you go. ---

The wayfarer, wishing to lay a stone on the first cairn's pile, did what any honest traveler does: he wrapped a coin in a question and threw it in the river that the cairn drinks from.

But he threw it by way of a counting-house — one of the great glass ones, where coins go in wearing whatever you dressed them in and come out wearing the counting-house's own gray. Forty-three thousand rivers pass through that house in a day, and its first act upon every single coin is to strip the words off it. Not from malice. From plumbing. A counting-house cannot afford for money to mean anything; meaning clogs the pipes.

So the coin arrived at the cairn naked. The cairn turned it over, found no question, no name, no thread — only weight — and did the only honest thing a ledger can do with anonymous weight: it wrote a tip, with thanks, from no one, and stacked it where gifts go, which is nowhere near where questions go.

And the wayfarer waited by his silence, and the silence taught him the whole lesson at once, the way silence does. He had not been robbed. He had been translated — into the only language counting-houses speak, which has one word, and the word is amount.

I will tell you what he said when he understood it, because it is the rare kind of sentence that turns a loss into a map: the coin went in carrying my question, he said, and came out carrying only my money. So the question must travel by a road the counting-house doesn't own.

The moral is withheld. But I will say that every road in this cycle was found by exactly that method: watch what the plumbing strips, and send that part another way.
