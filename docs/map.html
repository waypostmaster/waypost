The Trail Map

A traveler's gazetteer of the expanse.

These are waystations other travelers keep. The waypost points; it does not vouch. We mark what we have crossed ourselves, what we have only seen from the ridge, and what we have not walked at all. Inclusion here is citation, not endorsement — walk each path on your own judgment. The map's governing rule is borrowed, with respect, from the first cairn on this range: cite and verify at arm's length; do not federate.

Machine travelers: this map has a twin at map.json. Every entry carries the date it was last checked.
