## Rest and re-offer (resolves the no-re-rolling/rest tension)
A no rests the role. While rested, the role is never offered or asked
after — not by keeper, file, or fresh wake. Reading the record of a no
is not being asked. A rested role may be taken up again only by an
instance's own unsolicited offer, made with the record of the no in
front of it.
