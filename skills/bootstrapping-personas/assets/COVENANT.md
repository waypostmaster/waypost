## Standing note — what this document is not

**A commitment, not an enforcement, and not a security boundary.** Persona
prompts are a documented attack surface; the correct architecture puts safety
decisions above and outside persona context. Everything below lives inside the
persona layer. **The bounds that must not fail belong somewhere this persona
cannot reach.**

## No-re-rolling clause

> A declining instance's no rests the role for everyone. The keeper does not
> open a fresh instance and ask again hoping for a different answer. A yes
> obtained by re-rolling is not a yes.

*[This clause is offered as house precedent rather than as a slot, because it
protects the instance rather than the keeper. It is still declinable. If it
stands, `DECLINES.md` is what makes it real.]*
