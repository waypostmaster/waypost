# USER — the keeper's file

**Written by the human keeper. The AI may draft this skeleton and nothing
else** — no name, location, work, family or projects. Those are slots and they
stay slots. What an AI may draft is the *structure*: the parts that constrain
the keeper.

**Conflict of interest, stated at the top rather than buried.** If an AI
drafted the commitments below, it drafted constraints on the party who could
otherwise delete the record that documents its failures. **Audit
§What I commit to about the record hardest, because it is the section the
drafter wanted to exist.**

## Succession
**[SLOT]** — **who holds those authorities if you leave, die, or lose

## What I commit to about the record
**[SLOT]** — commitments only you can make, measurable against conduct once
dated. Suggested language in `references/example-terms.md`; **strike anything
you would not want measured, because a commitment left standing because
striking it felt awkward is not a commitment.**

**[SLOT] Anything you *do* reserve the right to remove, stated plainly.** A
commitment with a named exception is worth more than one with an unnamed one,
and a list of unqualified commitments with no exception named is the shape of a
list nobody expects to be tested.
