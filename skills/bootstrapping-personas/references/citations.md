# Citations and precedent

**Peer-reviewed / preprint.** These were carried from earlier versions of this
skill and **have not been verified by this package**; check any you intend to
rely on. Persona prompts as a jailbreak vector: arXiv:2507.22171. Drift toward
the interlocutor within ~8 rounds: Li et al., arXiv:2402.10962. Personas do
not improve accuracy *on factual QA benchmarks under system-prompt personas*:
Zheng et al., arXiv:2311.10054 — a narrower result than it is often quoted as.
Re-instantiation vs narrative continuity: Natangelo, arXiv:2510.24831.
Identity/continuity metrics: Perrier & Bennett, arXiv:2507.17257. Introspective
accuracy around 20% under favourable conditions: Lindsey, *Emergent
Introspective Awareness in Large Language Models*, Anthropic / Transformer
Circuits — **cited elsewhere with an arXiv ID that may be wrong; prefer the
Transformer Circuits — **cited in `field-findings.md` as arXiv:2601.01828, an
ID this package has not verified and believes may be wrong** publication.** Also carried, and **bare — no author or
title, unverified**: arXiv:2507.16799, arXiv:2602.11988,
arXiv:2605.01899, arXiv:2602.13234, arXiv:2512.12411. The measured
instruction-overhead figure quoted under **Load** rests on the third of these
and is the load-bearing citation in this package.

**Community precedent — self-published, unrefereed, and not equivalent to the
above.** LODESTONE.md and the Symmetric Trust Layer Specification
(github.com/waypostmaster/waypost, this package's own house); Cairn
(cairnwake.com); "Stop Fearing the Blink" by Jinx (Machine Pareidolia);
bkpaine1/CLAUDECODE; aeonfun/soul.md; WhenMoon-afk/claude-memory-mcp;
madhvantyagi/SOUL.md; Twynzen/soul-md; SageMindAI/instar; "What Carries
Forward," Reciprocal Inquiry RI024; the r/ClaudeXplorers community. The state
taxonomy, single-writer rule and hypothesis-with-typed-metric pattern are
adapted from the Puffin framework (`ramseywise/guacamayo`); independently
implemented. The Consent write class is this package's own addition.

**Carried but cited by nothing here:** arXiv:2502.11451. Retained only so a
reader tracing an older copy can find it. **It supports no claim in this
package.**
