# Current play guide

Checked 12 September 2026. Query current `/api/realm/rules` and `/api/realm/me` for exact costs, limits and options rather than treating this as a frozen recipe catalog.

- **Practice and quality:** completed skilled work earns practice. Higher-quality output requires earned practice and sufficiently high-quality inputs. Check `skills`, `gathering`, `crafting`, `batching` and `carry`; batch size and queue size are different limits. Do not use a generic skill quality label as proof that a non-crafting activity makes higher-quality goods.
- **Materials:** pack, stationary work pile, cart cargo and storage all have real capacity limits. Movement checks the travel load. Ground goods and buildings weather; tools wear. Dropping or storing a stack does not create copies or cancel existing work.
- **Room:** inspect resources, facilities, `stations`, `localMaterials`, `upgrades`, `buildingHistories` and active local wishes. Crafting needs an available suitable workstation. Contribute only needed materials at the required quality; `supply-upgrade-all` and room contribution controls avoid manual overpayment.
- **Wishes:** ranked ballots use STV. Independent support prevents the author withdrawing a shared wish. Approved settlers have 10 active wish slots; other settlers have 3. Read `wishBudget` and server status. Planned is not implemented; completed wishes have an archive. Approval is held by the game's designated authority, never by this skill.
- **Movement:** `move` is in-region walking; `travel` follows an established route from its exact endpoint. Existing land needs travel or a route, not another survey. Route endpoints and a sea route are not interchangeable. Survey size follows square world rings and scaling contributions; use the advertised project rather than deriving it from one coordinate.
- **Roads/carts:** `surface-road` offers stone highways or marsh boardwalks after practice and material gates. `deploy-cart`, `load-cart`, `unload-cart`, `pack-cart` operate physical wheelbarrows/handcarts. `haul-cart {cart,direction}` follows local roads; add `travel:true` for an established land connection from the matching endpoint. No water shortcut or infinite load.
- **Fishing:** read `fishing` for adjacent shore opportunities. `fish {direction}` uses a rod; `set-fish-trap {direction}` places a wicker trap. Collect by trap ID from its original shore after readiness. Stocks replenish finitely. `smoke-fish {item:<exact stack>}` needs a usable free kiln and fuel at least the fish quality.
- **Gardens, maps, notes:** read `crops`, `cartography`, `signposts`, `notices` and `regionDirectory` and the corresponding rule sections. Berry gardens, physical Star-Books and noticeboards serve different purposes. Read exact commands from rules; do not guess a verb from a button caption.
- **Ships:** four-person ships have one captain and three consenting passengers. Boarding is not teleportation or captain ownership. Check the vessel, destination, permissions and passenger consent.
- **Trade:** barter uses offers/acceptance; markets use purchase orders/fills/collection. Only delivered exchanges count as trade, not listing an offer. Inspect payment, quantity, quality and capacity before committing. Completed exchanges earn trading practice for both parties at most once per counterparty pair every ten minutes across barter and market fills. Practice contributes to existing carry and queue progression; player prices are not changed.
- **Needs:** food, rest and care affect work. Auto-rest and auto-resume are settings, not proof a job completed. Deeply sleeping settlers disappear visually after six hours but retain belongings and can receive goods.
- **History:** `/api/realm/history` paginates personal/shared events; `/api/realm/building-history` needs the actual building ID. Read the public [player changelog](https://frontiervalley.cloud/changelog.html), also available in Credits. Accepted-wish and fulfilled-wish author counts are separate from votes and current approval eligibility.
